TASK: Cross-sell targeting model + back-test (Retail Bank - Marketing request)

Request: Marketing contacts 4,000 customers per campaign and converts ~1 in 9 at random. Build a model of who will take up another product within 90 days, explain why, and prove the value: back-test which 4,000 customers it would have picked on 30 June and how many actually converted vs random. Package it to score the 30 Sep snapshot for Q4.

Inputs (Deliverables folder):
  input 1 - customers.csv (20,000 customers, 30 Jun 2026 snapshot, outcome Jul-Sep)
  input 2 - data dictionary.xlsx
  input 3 - campaign brief.docx (4,000 contacts, $4/contact, $150 margin, compliance rules)

Plan:
  1. Read brief + dictionary, note constraints and leakage suspects
  2. Clean data, audit leakage, stratified train/test split
  3. Logistic baseline -> gradient boosting -> PR-AUC, lift, calibration
  4. SHAP drivers -> messaging guidance
  5. Out-of-fold scores, filter (opt-in, <2 complaints), top 4,000 -> back-test ROI in Excel
  6. Package model + README, reproduce, 3-slide summary

Done when: hold-out ROC-AUC >= 0.78, beats baseline PR-AUC, top-20% lift >= 2.5x, no leaky/gender fields, 4,000 compliant rows converting >= 2x the eligible average, reproducible.
